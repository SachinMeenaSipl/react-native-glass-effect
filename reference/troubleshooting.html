Troubleshooting ​

Find your symptom by area. Each row says what causes it, how to fix it, and how to check the fix.

First step on Android: adb logcat -s LiquidGlass. Most setup problems print a one-line explanation there.

"Invalid hook call" when the library is linked from a local folderA second copy of React is loaded from the library's own node_modulesResolve react, react-native and the library from your app. See Expo → local folderThe warning is gone
